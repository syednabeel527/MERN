Vanilla Cupcakes

A light and fluffy vanilla cupcake recipe for beginners.

Perfect for tea parties and gatherings!

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp nutmeg (optional)
  • 1/2 cup butter cubes
  • 2 large eggs

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190oC.
  2. Gently whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. Add butter and eggs, whisk until creamy.
  4. Bake for 20 minutes or until the tops bounce back.

Tips

For extra sweetness, add berry jam inside the center.
You can also top with buttercream frosting for a classic look!
